# README.md

# Guardian

> **An Explainable Trust Intelligence Platform**

Guardian is an AI-powered Trust Intelligence platform that helps people and organizations determine whether a digital opportunity can be trusted **before** they commit money, personal information, or time.

Unlike traditional fraud detection systems that react after a loss has occurred, Guardian investigates digital interactions, explains the evidence it finds, and provides transparent recommendations based on observed facts.

---

# Mission

Guardian exists to make trust measurable.

Every day, people encounter opportunities that appear legitimate but may hide fraud, manipulation, or exploitation.

Guardian investigates those opportunities using explainable AI and transforms complex digital evidence into clear, actionable intelligence.

---

# Vision

We believe the future of digital safety is not built on detecting scams alone.

It is built on measuring trust.

Guardian is creating a new category:

**Trust Intelligence**

---

# Core Principles

- Evidence before conclusions
- Explainability before automation
- Behaviour before keywords
- Transparency before confidence
- Human judgement remains essential

Guardian never simply declares that something is a scam.

Guardian presents evidence, evaluates competing hypotheses, explains its reasoning, and helps users make informed decisions.
