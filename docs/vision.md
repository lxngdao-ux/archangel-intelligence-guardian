# Vision

Archangel Intelligence Guardian (AAIG) is an AI-powered Trust Intelligence platform that helps people and organizations evaluate digital opportunities before committing money, personal information, or time.

Our mission is to make trust measurable.

Rather than reacting to fraud after it occurs, AAIG predicts trustworthiness before users act.

Core capabilities include:

- Scam Detection
- Website Risk Analysis
- Company Verification
- Investment Verification
- Marketplace Safety
- Explainable AI Trust Scores
- Community Intelligence
